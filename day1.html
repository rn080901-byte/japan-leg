Arrival Day Logic
You left Sydney this morning and you've been in transit for the best part of 12 hours. The goal tonight is simple: land safely, get to the hotel, and let Osaka give you its first small gift — or nothing at all, if the body says no. One short walk, one street snack, one good sleep. The city has three full days ahead. Tonight you just arrive.

Before You Leave Sydney
Pre-trip
10 min
Set Up Mobile Suica on Apple Wallet Prep
¥2,000 loaded ~A$21 each

This is the single biggest friction reduction you can make for Day 1. Open Apple Wallet → + → Transit Card → Suica. Load ¥2,000–3,000 per phone with your credit card. Both of you set this up before you fly.

Suica works identically to ICOCA on every train, subway, bus and konbini in Japan — you'll never need to queue at a ticket machine. Top up from the Wallet app whenever the balance runs low.

📱
If either of you is on Android, Google Wallet supports Suica in some regions but it's patchy outside Japan. In that case, buy an IC card (ICOCA or Pasmo) on arrival — but only for the Android phone. One person queueing is better than two.
On Arrival
19:50
Land
Touch Down — Kansai International Airport Transit
¥0 Free

Wheels down at KIX — built on an artificial island in Osaka Bay. Follow the crowd toward Immigration. The terminal is modern, well signed in English, and completely straightforward.

💡
Have your passport and Visit Japan Web QR code ready before you reach the counter. Biometrics (fingerprints + photo) are taken from all non-Japanese visitors — quick, about 30 seconds each.
20:00
45–60 min
Immigration · Baggage · Walk to Nankai Transit
¥0 Free

Expect 30–60 minutes for immigration on a full evening arrival — JQ13 usually lands around other long-haul flights. Don't fight the queue. Stretch, hydrate, breathe.

Collect bags. Walk directly to the Nankai Railway station, which is underground and connected to the terminal via a covered walkway. Because your Suica is already set up, you walk straight past the ticket machines.

⚠️
Realistic boarding window for your train is 21:00–21:20, not 20:40. Build this into your expectations. If you beat it, great. If not, nothing is lost — the next train is always 15–30 minutes away.
21:10
38–48 min
Nankai Train — KIX → Namba Transit
¥1,940–2,900 ~A$20–30

You have a choice here. On a fresh morning, the Rapi:t's novelty wins. At 9pm after 12 hours of travel, the cheaper regular express may be the better call.

🤖
Option A · Rapi:t Limited Express
¥1,450pp · 38 min · Reserved seat · Silver robot-nose train · Every ~30 min
🚃
Option B · Nankai Airport Express
¥970pp · 45–48 min · Unreserved · More frequent · Just tap your Suica and board
💭
Honest call: if you're still buzzing and want the "arrival ceremony" feel, take the Rapi:t. If you're glazed over and just want a seat, the Airport Express with no ticket-machine faff is the kinder choice. Either way you're in Namba by 22:00.
22:00
15 min
Namba Station → Konbini → Hiyori Hotel Walk
¥500–800 ~A$5–8

Take the South Exit from Namba. Walk 6 minutes south along Midosuji-suji. There is a Lawson or 7-Eleven literally on this walk — stop once, grab everything you need for the room, keep moving. One stop, not two.

🍙
Konbini run: 2× onigiri · 2× cold green tea · 1L cold water · Lawson egg salad sandwich for tomorrow's breakfast · melon pan as a reward. Total ~¥800. Japan's konbini are extraordinary — this is not a consolation prize.
22:15
20 min
Check In — Hiyori Hotel Osaka Namba Hotel
Incl. Pre-paid

Check in, take the lift up, drop the bags. Open the curtains. Osaka will be glowing outside. Shower first. Change into something light. You've been travelling for 12+ hours — your body needs the reset signal before anything else.

🛁
Hiyori Namba rooms have a standard Japanese business-hotel unit bath — a deep-ish shower-over-tub, not a traditional ofuro. Still fine for a hot soak tonight. The proper cedar-tub soaking reset is waiting for you in the Kyoto ryokan later in the trip.
22:45
30 min
Dotonbori First Glimpse — Only If Energy Allows Optional
~¥500 ~A$5

This is a "maybe" not a "plan." If you're both freshly showered and wide awake, walk 8 minutes to Dotonbori. You'll smell it before you see it — frying batter, grilled meat, canal air. Then the neon hits: the giant Glico Running Man, the mechanical crab, the canal reflecting everything in double. Stand on Ebisu-bashi bridge. Look both ways. Let the city introduce itself.

🐙
One thing only: a small tray of takoyaki (~¥500) from any street stall. Eat it on the bridge. That's your first real Osaka moment. Don't complicate it.
⚠️
If either of you hesitates for even two seconds when asked "do you want to go?" — the answer is no. Osaka will still be there at 08:00 tomorrow. Day 2 is the main event; don't mortgage it for a photo tonight.
23:30
All night
Sleep — Hard Stop Rest
Incl. Pre-paid

Lights out by 23:30 at the latest. Set two alarms for 07:30 — Day 2 starts with Osaka Castle at 09:00 and you need breakfast, a slow coffee, and an unhurried walk to the station first. Rushing Day 2 undoes the careful pacing of Day 1.

Osaka is 1 hour behind Sydney in August. You may wake earlier than planned — don't fight it. Lie still, drink the water, watch the city wake up from the window.

📋
Before you sleep: phones charging · SPF 50 accessible on the desk · water bottles filled · Suica balance checked · light clothes laid out. Morning brain doesn't organise well.
